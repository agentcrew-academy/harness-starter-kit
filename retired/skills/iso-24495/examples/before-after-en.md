# Before / After — English

Each pair names the principle that drove the rewrite.

## 1. Front-load the point (Findable)

**Before**
> In light of recent changes to our internal processes, and after careful
> consideration of the various options available to us, we have made the
> decision that, effective from the beginning of next month, the weekly
> status meeting will be moved from Monday morning to Wednesday afternoon.

**After**
> From next month, the weekly status meeting moves to Wednesday afternoon
> (was: Monday morning).

## 2. Actors and deadlines (Usable)

**Before**
> It is expected that the reimbursement form should be completed and
> submitted in a timely manner in order for processing to be initiated.

**After**
> Submit your reimbursement form by the 25th. We start processing it the
> day we receive it.

## 3. Nominalization and hedge stacks (Understandable)

**Before**
> The team will make a determination as to whether an implementation of the
> proposed solution may potentially be feasible in some circumstances.

**After**
> The team will decide whether the proposed solution is feasible.

Kept as-is: the decision itself is still open — "whether" carries that.

## 4. Cut what the reader does not need (Relevant)

**Before**
> Our company was founded in 2011 with a mission to revolutionize customer
> communication. We take every piece of feedback seriously and are committed
> to continuous improvement. Regarding your report: the sync error you saw
> is fixed in version 2.4.1.

**After**
> The sync error you reported is fixed in version 2.4.1. Update your app to
> get the fix.

## 5. One idea per sentence (Understandable)

**Before**
> If the export fails, which can happen when the file exceeds the size limit
> that was configured by your administrator, an error will be shown and the
> partial file that was created during the attempt will be removed.

**After**
> The export fails if the file exceeds your administrator's size limit.
> You get an error message. The tool deletes the partial file it created.
